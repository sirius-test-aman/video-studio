Write this once per module. Keep it under two pages.

## What it does

One paragraph, plain language, no marketing. What a user can do with this module
that they could not do before.

## Who it is for

The specific role, not "everyone". Which team, which part of their week, what
they are doing immediately before and after they would touch this.

## The adoption blocker

**The most important section.** Why are people not already using this? Pick one:

- They do not know it exists
- They do not believe the output will be good enough
- They do not trust an AI to touch this artifact
- It looks like more work than the manual way
- They tried it once and it disappointed them
- It is not in their existing workflow

A promo's whole argument should answer this. Name it explicitly, because a video
that argues against the wrong blocker will look fine and land badly.

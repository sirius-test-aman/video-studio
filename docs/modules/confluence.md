# Module: Confluence

Lists the BRDs that have been pushed to the project's linked Confluence space,
and is where the Planning flow starts.

---

## What it does

Shows BRDs pushed to Confluence, lets you open and read them inside Velox, and
carries the **Generate Jira Stories** action that starts the Planning flow.

## Who it is for

**Business Users** get full access; **Tech Users** read-only. In practice it is
used by whoever owns the requirement and whoever builds the backlog from it.

## Does this module warrant its own video?

**Not on its own.** Its two genuine value points belong to other videos:

- **Two-way sync** is worth one line inside a BRD Assistant video.
- **Generate Jira Stories** is the opening of a Planning video.

## What is genuinely true

- **Two-way sync**: changes made on the Confluence page are reflected in the Velox Confluence tab.
- Each project is linked to one Confluence space at project creation.
- A pushed BRD lands on a newly created page and Velox returns the link.

## What we do not claim

- Do not describe the sync as instant or continuous without qualification.
- Do not imply Velox is a Confluence editor.

# Module: Jira (view only)

A read-only view of the Epics and Stories that the Planning flow created in the
linked Jira project.

---

## What it does

Lists created Jira issues for the project. You land here automatically after
Create in Jira. Nothing is created or edited from this tab.

## Who it is for

Anyone verifying what was created. **Business Users** get it as part of their
role bundle; **Tech Users** get it read-only.

## Does this module warrant its own video?

**No.** It is a verification surface, not a value proposition. Trying to promo it
would produce a video about looking at a list.

It should appear as the **closing frame** of a Planning video, where it proves the
work landed. See `docs/modules/planning.md`.

## What is genuinely true

- Shows Epics and Stories created from a BRD, with their Epic links.
- You are redirected here after Create in Jira.
- Read-only. Editing happens in Jira itself.

## What we do not claim

- Never present it as a Jira replacement or a place to manage work.
- Never imply issues can be created from this tab.
